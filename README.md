# Padmini Janaki — Digital Identity Hub

A minimal, prose-focused digital identity hub for Padmini Janaki — health tech founder, CEO & Co-founder of Mind & Mom, author, and active voice in women's health.

## Design Philosophy
This is a **digital identity hub**, not a portfolio. In 30 seconds, a stranger can understand who she is, what she's built, and where to find her thinking. The design is intentionally minimal — it exists to make content effortless to scan and read.

## Visual Direction
- **Background**: Warm off-white (`#FDF8F3`). Dark mode: `#121212`.
- **Text**: Near-black (`#1A1A1A`). Dark mode: `#E8E4DE`.
- **Accent**: Muted terracotta (`#C75B4A`), used exclusively for 1px link hover underlines.
- **Typography**: Editorial humanist serif (*Newsreader* / *Source Serif 4*), size 18–19px desktop (17px mobile), line-height 1.7.
- **Max Content Width**: 620px, centered.
- **Zero Distraction**: No images, no icons, no cards, no borders, no buttons, no gradients, no shadows.

## Structure
1. **Name**: Padmini Janaki
2. **Identifier**: `health tech · AI · wearables · writer`
3. **Positioning Paragraph**: Concise trajectory capturing her Madras roots, Mind & Mom reach (2 lakh+ women), and book authorship.
4. **Written**: Books & Essays (Google Play Books, LinkedIn, publication placeholders).
5. **Spoken**: Reverse chronological podcasts & talks (*Time Billionaire's Club*, *TEDxChennai Institute of Technology*, *Dare2Begin*, *The JM Talk Show*, *Product Talk with Malthi*, *I Love Sarees*).
6. **Elsewhere**: LinkedIn, Instagram, Email.
7. **Footer**: © 2026 Padmini Janaki, Privacy.

## Interactions
- **Page Load**: Soft 6px fade-up over 500ms, staggered 60ms per block.
- **Link Hover**: 1px terracotta underline draws in from the left over 250ms (ease-out) with zero color or weight change.
- **Accessibility**: 2px terracotta focus outline (`offset: 2px`), contrast ratio > 7:1.

## Tech Stack
- React 19 + TypeScript
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Motion (Framer Motion load animation only)
